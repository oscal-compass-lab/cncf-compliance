# Privateer check coverage

Generated by [`scripts/privateer_mapping.py`](../scripts/privateer_mapping.py). Do not edit by hand.

Every control in the `cncf-repo` profile (the controls in scope for git repositories) is checked by its own Privateer requirement, or has no check for the stated reason. Requirements come from two plugins: `github-repo`, which evaluates the [OSPS Baseline](https://baseline.openssf.org), and our own `cncf-controls` plugin ([`privateer-plugins/cncf-controls`](../privateer-plugins/cncf-controls)), which covers the five controls the Baseline cannot. No requirement is used by more than one control.

- **Controls in scope:** 55
- **With a unique Privateer check:** 22 (8 exact, 14 partial)
- **Without a unique Privateer check:** 33

A partial mapping means a pass is necessary but not sufficient evidence for the control. A control without a check is reported as not determined and never counts as a pass.
